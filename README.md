## Research Experience & Selected Projects

### 1. Clinical Outcome Prediction Using Machine Learning
**CHRU Nancy, France | Machine Learning Research Internship**

**Fields:** Applied Machine Learning, Biostatistics, Clinical Prediction

Investigated the prediction of postoperative recovery following surgery for primary hyperparathyroidism using clinical data.

The study involved:
- Preprocessing and harmonization of heterogeneous clinical measurements
- Statistical analysis and feature evaluation
- Comparison of Logistic Regression and Random Forest models
- Cross-validation and bootstrap-based performance assessment
- Analysis of predictive uncertainty, decision thresholds, and model interpretability

**Key finding:** Random Forest achieved a ROC-AUC of approximately 0.76, although the limited cohort size and absence of external validation prevented clinical deployment.

*Patient-level data and original computational materials are not publicly shared due to confidentiality considerations.*
